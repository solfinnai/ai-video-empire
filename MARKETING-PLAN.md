### Hook Formula Breakdown

**1. Bold Claim Hook**
- Structure: [Outrageous claim] + [Proof element] + [Curiosity gap]
- Example: "I made $47K last month with AI videos (and you can too)"

**2. Question Hook**
- Structure: [Provocative question] + [Personal context] + [Value promise]
- Example: "What if I told you AI could replace your entire video team?"

**3. PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solution)**
- Problem: "Video creation takes forever"
- Agitate: "While you're editing for hours, competitors are shipping content"
- Solution: "Here's how AI cuts that time by 90%"

**4. AIDA (Attention-Interest-Desire-Action)**
- Attention: Bold opener
- Interest: Unique angle
- Desire: Benefit/outcome
- Action: "Watch till the end"

**5. Reason Why**
- Structure: "Here's why [interesting claim]" + [3 specific reasons]
- Example: "Here's why AI videos convert 3x better than traditional ones"
